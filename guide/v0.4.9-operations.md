# HunterX v0.4.9 operations guide

## Before a known sale time

Use on-sale mode and enter the target as `YYYY/MM/DD HH:MM:SS.mmm`, for example
`2026/08/10 11:00:00.000`. HunterX interprets it as Asia/Taipei, preserves the
milliseconds and converts it once to a monotonic one-shot deadline.

1. In Windows **Date & time**, enable automatic time and timezone, select
   Taipei, and press **Sync now**.
2. Start early, open the correct event page and confirm the visible account is
   signed in.
3. Recheck date/area keywords, exclusions, count, fallback and
   `allow_less_tickets`; leave allow-less off unless fewer tickets are wanted.
4. Confirm no visible waiting room, challenge or disconnected browser state.
5. Do not change the system clock during the final arming window.

At the deadline one guarded reload is requested. A queue/waiting room remains
protected and may intentionally prevent that request. Local clock error,
browser scheduling, network latency and a site's own opening time are outside
the process and should not be described as guaranteed zero latency.

On a TixCraft activity-detail page, the configured safe-page interval continues
until the real purchase entry is rendered. HunterX then enters the game/date
page. On `/ticket/area` it applies area keywords first and then the configured
ordering policy; keep both date and area auto-selection enabled in the profile.

## Leak-watch operation

Use leak-watch for sporadic releases and clearing events. The configured leak
interval is a deterministic minimum on classified safe pages, not a random
sleep. One completed no-ticket scan is allowed per document generation. A new
reload creates a new generation; finding a candidate cancels pending refresh
and enters the same purchase handler used by on-sale mode.

Do not use aggressive one-second intervals to work around a white page. A
confirmed TixCraft-family soft block owns one immutable configured/default wait;
ordinary refresh remains suppressed until its recovery deadline. Queue,
challenge, TICKET, ORDER, CHECKOUT and PAYMENT never become leak-refresh targets.

## Important diagnostics

- `[REFRESH] dispatch` reports priority, generation and scheduled lateness.
- `[REFRESH] suppressed` reports minimum interval, coalescing, soft-block,
  single-flight or purchase protection.
- `[EPS BLOCK]` reports suspected, confirmed wait and recovery transitions.
- `[TIXCRAFT AREA PERF]`, `[TIXCRAFT TICKET PERF]` and `[TIXCRAFT PERF]`
  report bounded stage latency when verbose diagnostics are enabled.
- `[URL DIAG] safe_stop` means persistent empty URL/CDP state stopped safely;
  inspect the browser and restart the affected instance manually.

Logs, `settings.json`, instance profiles and backups may contain sensitive
account configuration even though known fields are redacted or masked. Do not
publish them. Manual CAPTCHA, waiting-room/challenge and payment handoffs remain
intentional safety boundaries.
